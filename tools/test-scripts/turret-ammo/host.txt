# Scenario "turret-ammo" (Remote Turret, ammo with reserve and reload): 2 copies. Runs
# after lobby-host.txt. The [turret] "clip ... reserve" lines show the ammo:
#   1. give: clip 250, reserve 500;
#   2. throw a short way ahead: the turret gets 249, the gun reloads from the reserve;
#   3. walk up to the turret and use it: its rounds go back into the reserve;
#   4. throw again, take control, fire ~2 s, tap Activate/Reload: the turret is topped up
#      to 254 from the clip and the reserve after 1.5 s;
#   5. leave.

wait 2500

log step 2: throw

wait 4000                    # the gun reloads meanwhile
log step 3: pick it up

wait 500
face turret
wait 300

wait 1500

log step 4: throw, control, fire, reload
wait 2000

wait 2500

wait 2000
